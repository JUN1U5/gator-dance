## Point Tracking
Below are the point tracking systems. You may back date for your time at Green Level or if you are new to us, you may back date to the June before you attend Green Level High School! You need 30 points required for induction, 20 of which must be earned through participation in chapter site program activities. Go ahead and keep track of this so when we can meet as a club we can plan for inductions. Please remember you must be an active member of the Green Level Dance Department to partake in National Dance Honors Society. 

**Reminder**: once you apply to NHSDA at ​https://www.nhsda-ndeo.org/content.aspx?page_id=1126&club_id=661544 you will also need to complete all requirements for induction on the NHSDA Google Classroom. Please see Mrs. Pope if you need access to the Google Classroom.
