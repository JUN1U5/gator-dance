Hello Parents/Guardians,

I am so excited to have your child as one of my students.  It is my belief that including the arts in your child's education is necessary and I thank you for allowing them to be apart of the Green Level Dance Department. This page will act as an updates page for you. I will post Daily Agendas for each class along with any important announcements. Please feel free to reach out to me with any questions. You may also have your student add you as a parent/guardian to our Google Classroom in the semester that they take dance. This will give you weekly updates on your student's progress.

Thank you,

Andrea Dula
